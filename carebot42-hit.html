✦ CAREBOT-42 · Data for Domestic & Presentation Work
You will direct a robot to perform work done by humanoid robots — domestic labor or presentation labor.
Right now humans are wearing cameras on their heads and getting paid to film themselves doing domestic work in order to train the robots of the future.

The instruction you build is saved as data. It is visible on the CAREtech live results dashboard. The data feeds the system.
✦ How this works
Build the instruction. The data is saved. You are done. The robot performs. You get paid.
Step 1: Tap the spinning image to select a robot from the performance.
Step 2: Type the name of the object you see in the image.
Step 3: Describe the task being completed in the image.
Step 4: Build the instruction — choose 4 actions, how many times, 3 locations to move within the 7 ft × 7 ft square, and whether the object is on or off.
Step 5: A diagram of the movements and spoken instruction are generated. Your instruction is saved as data to the live dashboard.
