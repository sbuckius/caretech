✦ CAREBOT-42 · Nonsensical Data for Domestic Work
You are the CEO. You are directing a robot to perform domestic labor. The instructions you generate are the data.
This task is about domestic work — the labor of the home: laundry, dishes, cooking, cleaning, caregiving. This work is invisible in economic data, undervalued in the market, and now being automated by robots designed by people who have never done it.

You are taking the role of CEO. You will build an instruction for a robot to perform domestic tasks on stage. The instruction is intentionally absurd — a robot turning a laundry basket upside down three times, or placing a vacuum on its own head — because the premise is absurd. The premise is that a CEO directing a robot is the solution to the invisible labor that holds up the economy.

You do not deliver this instruction. The instruction you build is saved as data. It is visible on the CAREtech live results dashboard. The data feeds the system. The system does not know the data is nonsense. This is the point.
✦ How this works
Build the instruction. The data is saved. You are done.
Step 1: Tap the spinning image to select a robot from the performance.
Step 2: Type what you see in the image — describe the domestic task depicted.
Step 3: Build the instruction: choose an action, how many times, where on stage, and whether the object is on or off.
Step 4: A stage diagram and spoken instruction are generated. Your instruction is saved as data to the live dashboard.
You do not go to the robot. You do not deliver this instruction. The instruction lives in the database. The robot does not receive it. The work remains undone.
