✦ CAREBOT-41 · Research Briefing
Research says mothers and tech workers need the same skills. Who gets paid for them?
The research on what mothers must be skilled at — problem-solving under pressure, emotional regulation, multitasking, adaptive prioritisation, communication across developmental stages, crisis management, long-term planning, building trust and psychological safety — maps almost exactly onto what tech employers say they want from engineers and product teams in 2025.

This task shows you 16 skills identified by researchers in both fields. For each one, tell us: does this apply to mothers, to tech workers, to both, or to neither? Your responses build a live map of the overlap — as you and other participants see it.
